# EnergCalc

**EnergCalc helps a household find out what backup power it needs to get through a blackout — and explains every number in plain language.**

People tell EnergCalc what must stay on (fridge, internet, lights) and for how long. EnergCalc calculates energy, power, battery, inverter and solar panels; compares a **ready-made power station** with a **custom-built system**; and delivers a **technical report** with a shopping list, compatibility checks and a budget.

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they get |
|---|---|
| **Households and small businesses** | A clear answer to "how much battery do I need?" without having to understand electrical engineering. |
| **Technical readers** | Formulas, assumptions and limits stated openly, so every result can be checked. |
| **The product owner** | A dashboard with traffic, the sales funnel, sign-ups and purchases. |

## What the product does

- **Quick calculator** — appliance buttons and an hours slider; instant results.
- **Full calculator** — an 8-step wizard with a short "teacher" explanation at every step.
- **Free result** — energy, power, starting surge and a recommended ready-made power station.
- **Full report (paid)** — three configurations, a comparison, a bill of materials, compatibility checks and a PDF.
- **Buyer portal** — email and password access, the report on screen and a PDF download.
- **Owner dashboard** — metrics, journey tracking, sign-ups, purchases and reviews.

## Principles

1. **Electrical safety over price.** A cheaper option that is not safe is never shown.
2. **Nothing is made up.** Manufacturer data that hasn't been confirmed is labeled "unverified"; missing data shows as "insufficient data" — never as "compatible".
3. **Reproducible calculations.** The same inputs always give the same result, and every result lists its assumptions.
4. **Plain language without hiding limits.** The report is preliminary planning, not an engineered design signed by a licensed professional.
