# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **W (watt)** | Power: how hard an appliance draws energy right now. |
| **Wh / kWh (watt-hour / kilowatt-hour)** | Energy: what an appliance uses over time. 1 kWh = 1,000 Wh. |
| **Ah (amp-hour)** | A battery's charge at a given voltage. Only meaningful with the voltage: 100 Ah at 12 V ≈ 1.2 kWh. |
| **V (volt)** | Electrical voltage. Brazilian outlets: 127 V or 220 V. Battery banks: 12, 24 or 48 V. |
| **A (amp)** | Electrical current. Higher currents need thicker cables. |
| **Duty cycle** | The share of time an appliance actually draws power (e.g., a fridge compressor). |
| **Starting surge** | The extra power a motor draws for an instant when it starts. |
| **Inverter** | Converts battery energy (direct current) into outlet energy (alternating current). |
| **Pure sine** | A waveform identical to the grid's; required by motors and sensitive electronics. |
| **Apparent power (VA)** | Power including the power factor; matters for motors. |
| **Power factor** | How much apparent power becomes useful work (1 = all of it). |
| **Power station** | Battery, inverter and charger in a single portable box. |
| **LiFePO4** | Lithium iron phosphate battery: many cycles, deep discharge, safer chemistry. |
| **Lead-acid / AGM** | Traditional battery: cheaper and heavier, uses only about half its capacity. |
| **Depth of discharge (DoD)** | How much of a battery's capacity can be used safely. |
| **BMS** | The circuit that protects a battery from current, voltage and temperature outside its limits. |
| **Battery bank** | Several batteries wired in series (more voltage) and/or parallel (more capacity). |
| **Wp (watt-peak)** | A solar panel's power under standard test conditions. |
| **PSH (peak sun hours)** | How many hours of "full" sun a location gets per day, on average. |
| **MPPT** | A charge controller that gets the most out of the panels and charges the battery correctly. |
| **Voc / Vmp** | Panel voltage open-circuit / at maximum power. Both vary with temperature. |
| **Array (series × parallel)** | How panels are wired together. |
| **Transfer switch** | A device that stops inverter power from flowing back into the grid. |
| **Autonomy** | How long the system can power the loads without recharging. |
| **ART** | Brazil's professional liability certificate, issued by a licensed engineer or technician. |
