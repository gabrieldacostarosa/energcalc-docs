# Releases and roadmap

## History

| Release | Highlights |
|---|---|
| **0.3** | Welcome offer; verified-purchase reviews. |
| **0.2** | Owner dashboard with metrics, funnel and tracking; purchase via payment links; email-and-password portal; inverter-sizing fix for occasional-use appliances. |
| **0.1** | Calculation engine with tests; quick and full calculators; free result; on-screen and PDF report; initial Brazilian catalog. |

## Roadmap

- Sourced price quotes for every catalog product.
- Review of the Brazilian rules by a licensed electrical engineer.
- Solar irradiation data per municipality, replacing the regional estimate.
- Time-of-day usage scenarios (day and night).
- Grid-tied hybrid systems.
- Full English version of the technical texts and profiles for other countries.

## About these docs

These docs describe how EnergCalc works at a high level and in plain language. They follow their own style guide: short sentences, active voice, one idea per paragraph, technical terms defined on first use and collected in the [Glossary](#en-glossary), and examples with real numbers from the engine. Security details that could make abuse easier are not published.
