# Frequently asked questions

**Is the free result reliable?**
Yes. It uses the same calculation engine as the report. The report adds a comparison, a bill of materials, detailed compatibility checks and a PDF.

**Why is the suggested battery bigger than my consumption?**
Because no battery should be drained to zero, the inverter loses energy as heat and batteries lose capacity over time. The [Worked example](#en-worked-example) shows each factor.

**Can I use a car battery?**
Not recommended. It is built to start an engine, not to discharge slowly for hours.

**Can I run an electric shower or an air conditioner?**
An electric shower (5,500 W or more) drains a home battery in minutes. A small inverter air conditioner is possible with a large bank — calculate first.

**Can I plug the inverter into a wall outlet?**
No. Without a professionally installed transfer switch, power can flow back into the grid and injure line workers.

**Does the report replace an engineer?**
No. It is automated preliminary planning without professional certification. It helps you understand, compare, buy safely and talk to a professional.

**Do the station purchase links influence the recommendation?**
No. The recommendation is technical; referral links are labeled as such and don't change the result.
