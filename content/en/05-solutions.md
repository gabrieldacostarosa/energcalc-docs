# Power station vs. custom system

EnergCalc always compares both approaches. Neither is "better" in every case.

| | Ready-made power station | Custom-built system |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Battery, inverter and charger in a single box | Separate batteries, inverter, controller, cables and protection |
| **Installation** | None: unbox and use | Requires a licensed professional |
| **Portability** | High | Low (fixed installation) |
| **Cost per kWh** | Higher | Lower for large systems |
| **Expansion** | Limited to what the manufacturer offers | Broad, within component limits |
| **Sweet spot** | Up to ~2–3 kWh | Several days of autonomy, with solar |

## How the recommendation is chosen

- If you asked for a **ready-made station** or **portability**, the station is recommended whenever it fits.
- If they asked for a **custom** or **solar** system, the balanced configuration is recommended.
- In **"not sure"** mode, the station is recommended when its cost is competitive; otherwise the custom system.
- If no catalog station covers the whole period, the **closest** one appears, with the hours it can run.

> **Commercial independence.** Referral (affiliate) links never change the technical result or the order of recommendations. Whenever a referral link appears, it is labeled as such.

## The catalog

The catalog starts small and documented: power stations from well-known brands, batteries, inverters, panels, MPPT controllers and protection (fuses, cables, disconnects, transfer switch).

Every product has a **verification status**:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Unverified | Incomplete data, or not yet confirmed against the official document. |
| Manufacturer documented | Specs backed by an official page or datasheet. |
| Certification documented | A certification document was identified (per country). |
| Verified | Specs and documents reviewed. |
| Discontinued / Recalled / Rejected | Excluded from recommendations. |

**Catalog rules:**

- **Missing specs stay missing.** A surge rating the manufacturer doesn't publish shows as insufficient data — and that station isn't recommended for loads with motors.
- **Prices only with a source and date.** An item without a quote shows "no price on file" instead of an estimate.
- **Certifications are not interchangeable.** Brazil's Inmetro, UL and CE each apply to their own markets.
- **Generic components** (reference battery, inverter, panel) are labeled as such, to show typical market specs.
